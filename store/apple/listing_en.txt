TITLE : MedAnki DZ — Medical Revision
SHORT DESCRIPTION (80) : Spaced-repetition medical revision for Algerian students. Offline, FR/AR/EN.

FULL DESCRIPTION :
MedAnki DZ is the revision app built for Algeria's medical students.
Powered by spaced-repetition science (FSRS-5), it schedules each card
at the exact moment you're about to forget it.

WHAT MAKES MEDANKI DIFFERENT
• 697+ verified, peer-reviewed cards organised by module (anatomy,
  histology, embryology, biochemistry, biophysics…)
• Offline-first: decks encrypted on your device, sync when the network
  returns — built for intermittent connectivity
• AI hints without the cloud: personalized hints computed by rules,
  without sending your data to an external LLM
• Guard-railed voice tutor: ask course questions out loud — systematic
  medical disclaimer, emergency detection (SAMU 115), never medical advice
• Adaptive FSRS weights: the algorithm tunes to your profile (struggling
  or strong) so you review at the right time, never too much
• Module-based mock exams with detailed corrections
• Streaks, XP and per-faculty leaderboards (opt-in, pseudonymous)
• Trilingual: French, Arabic, English

WHO IS IT FOR?
First- and second-year medical students in Algeria who want to turn
20 minutes a day into exam success.

PRIVACY
Your reviews stay on your device. AI features send no identifiable
personal data to external services by default. Full policy: see
PRIVACY.md in the repository.

MedAnki DZ is a revision tool. It is not medical advice.
